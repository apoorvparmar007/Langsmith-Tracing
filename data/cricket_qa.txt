Q: Which Indian batsman is known as the "God of Cricket" and became the first player to score 100 international centuries?
A: Sachin Tendulkar.

Q: Who captained India from 2000 and rebuilt the team's confidence after the match-fixing scandal, later reaching the 2003 World Cup final?
A: Sourav Ganguly.

Q: Which player is nicknamed "The Wall" for his solid batting technique and later became India's head coach from 2021 to 2024?
A: Rahul Dravid.

Q: Who played the famous 281-run innings alongside Rahul Dravid against Australia at Kolkata in 2001?
A: VVS Laxman.

Q: Which bowler took all 10 wickets in a Test innings against Pakistan in Delhi in 1999 and is India's highest Test wicket-taker?
A: Anil Kumble.

Q: Who is known as the "Nawab of Najafgarh" and is the only Indian to score a Test triple century twice?
A: Virender Sehwag.

Q: Which captain led India to victory in the 2007 ICC World T20, the 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup, and the 2013 ICC Champions Trophy?
A: MS Dhoni.

Q: Who holds the record for most centuries in ODI cricket history and captained India from 2017 to 2022?
A: Virat Kohli.

Q: Which player holds the record for the highest individual score in ODI cricket (264) and captained India to the 2024 T20 World Cup title?
A: Rohit Sharma.

Q: Which fast bowler is known for his unusual bowling action and pinpoint yorkers, and has repeatedly topped ICC Test bowling rankings?
A: Jasprit Bumrah.

Q: Which all-rounder is known for his athletic fielding and left-arm orthodox spin, and was a key figure in India's Test and World Cup campaigns?
A: Ravindra Jadeja.

Q: Which spinner retired in 2024 as one of the fastest bowlers to reach 500 Test wickets?
A: Ravichandran Ashwin.

Q: Who hit six sixes in an over against England in the 2007 T20 World Cup and was Player of the Tournament in the 2011 World Cup?
A: Yuvraj Singh.

Q: Which fast bowler was the joint-highest wicket-taker in the 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup with 21 wickets?
A: Zaheer Khan.

Q: Who is known as the "Turbanator" and became the first Indian to take a Test hat-trick, against Australia in 2001?
A: Harbhajan Singh.

Q: Which player was the first Indian to score centuries in all three international formats (Test, ODI, T20I)?
A: Suresh Raina.

Q: Who top-scored in both the 2007 T20 World Cup final and the 2011 World Cup final for India?
A: Gautam Gambhir.

Q: Which opener, nicknamed "Gabbar," was Player of the Tournament at the 2013 ICC Champions Trophy?
A: Shikhar Dhawan.

Q: Which Indian cricketer is the only one to be awarded the Bharat Ratna?
A: Sachin Tendulkar (2014).

Q: What is the highest individual Test score by Virender Sehwag, and against which team was it scored?
A: 319 runs, against South Africa at Chennai in 2008.

Q: How many wickets did Harbhajan Singh take in the historic 2001 Test series against Australia?
A: 32 wickets.

Q: Which two ICC tournaments did Gautam Gambhir play a match-winning finishing role in, in terms of top-scoring in the final?
A: The 2007 ICC World T20 final and the 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup final.

Q: How many Test stumpings did MS Dhoni record, the most by an Indian wicketkeeper?
A: 123 Test stumpings.

Q: Which Indian bowler is known for mastering both conventional and reverse swing and led India's pace attack through the 2000s?
A: Zaheer Khan.
